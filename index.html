Dermatology Knowledge Base

Educate yourself on common skin conditions and fundamental skincare rules.

Acne Vulgaris

Caused by excess sebum, dead skin cells, and bacteria clogging hair follicles.

Derm Advice: Do not pop pimples. Use Salicylic Acid.

Eczema (Atopic Dermatitis)

A genetic condition that makes your skin red, severely itchy, and disrupts the barrier.

Derm Advice: Moisturize on damp skin. Avoid fragrances.

Hyperpigmentation

Dark patches resulting from excess melanin production due to sun damage or trauma.

Derm Advice: SPF 50 is non-negotiable. Use Vitamin C.
